Illustrative grade guidance

Age and grade placement can vary. The ranges below are examples only and are not official eligibility requirements.

  • Primary: Grades 1–5
  • Middle: Grades 6–8
  • Secondary: Grades 9–10
  • Senior Secondary: Grades 11–12

Sample document checklist

For a real school, confirm current requirements directly with that institution.

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous academic records, where applicable
  • Transfer certificate, where applicable
  • Recent student photographs
  • Parent or guardian contact details

A few common questions

Wondering about something?

Answers below describe a fictional example. Check with the real school for current information.

How can a family enquire about admissions?

Use the demonstration form above or contact the fictional admissions address. Neither route sends information.

Which grades are included in the sample programme?

The fictional school describes four stages, from Grade 1 through Grade 12. Grade ranges on this site are examples.

What documents might a school ask for?

A real school may request identity and previous academic records. The sample checklist above is illustrative, not an official requirement.

Is an interaction or assessment necessary?

The sample journey includes a possible age-appropriate interaction. This is not a real admissions policy.

Can families arrange a campus visit?

This fictional school has no live booking service. You can use the Contact page to explore its sample contact details.
