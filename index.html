A little about us

Every child deserves to feel they belong.

EAK International School is a fictional, inclusive K–12 learning community where academic growth and personal wellbeing go hand in hand.

We bring together caring educators, active discovery and a close-knit school culture. Our purpose is simple: help young people find their voice, build confidence and grow into thoughtful citizens.
