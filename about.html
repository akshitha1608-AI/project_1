Our story

Built on a simple belief: every child can flourish.

EAK International School is a fictional K–12 school imagined as a close-knit, future-facing community.

Established in 2008 within this sample story, EAK began with a wish to make learning feel both ambitious and deeply human. Today, our imagined school brings students, families and educators together around curiosity, care and a shared love of discovery.

We see education as more than preparation for exams. It is the steady work of building a voice, finding what matters and learning how to make a thoughtful contribution.

A welcoming place to learn and belong

Our vision

A future where every young person can imagine, contribute and thrive.

Our mission

To make purposeful learning a daily practice—rooted in curiosity, strengthened by care and open to possibility.

The values we practice

Small things make a school.

01 / CURIOSITY

Ask another question.

We make space to wonder, investigate and follow an idea beyond the obvious answer.

02 / KINDNESS

Make room for others.

Care begins with listening and grows through everyday choices to include one another.

03 / COURAGE

Try, learn, try again.

We value thoughtful risk-taking and the confidence to learn from a new challenge.

04 / INTEGRITY

Do the right thing.

Honesty, responsibility and respect guide the way we learn and live together.
